## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Record"**

Kinvox is a paper record of a parent's day, kept warm. The whole app sits on a cream
ground (Palette F, "Cornflower & Cream" — the name is in `index.css`) with exactly one
blue accent, and everything on it reads like entries in a ledger the adult child can
trust: doses confirmed, calls made, and — above everything — the parent's own words,
verbatim, in quotation marks. The user opens this app anxious; the design's job is that a
calm day looks calm (whitespace and one reassuring sentence) and a bad day is loud and
unmistakable (the alert card leads the Home screen and owns the full width), on the same
layouts.

The recurring motif is **the line**: one hairline is the call connecting a child in one
city to a parent in another. It is the wordmark's underline, the spine of the day thread,
the severity spine on a quote, and the rule between sections. Depth comes from stepped
cream tones and hairline borders far more than from shadow; the two shadows that exist are
warm-tinted (`rgba(58, 44, 20, …)`) so they never go blue-grey on cream.

The form language is softer than a ledger usually is. Cards are generously rounded (16px)
and buttons and chips are fully round — the record is warm stationery, not a spreadsheet.
Radius carries hierarchy: the further a surface is from the content, the softer it gets.

This is a medical-adjacent product with a safety posture: the system never decorates
meaning into colour alone, never invents a score, and never pretends the app itself can
dial a phone.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm cream neutral ramp (11 steps, `ink` → `paper`), one blue accent, status colours
  only ever layered on marks that already have shape and a text label.
- IBM Plex Sans everywhere; Newsreader serif reserved for care content (verbatim quotes,
  medicine names, hero lines, the wordmark); IBM Plex Sans Devanagari bound via
  `:lang(hi)` / `:lang(mr)` / `:lang(pa)`.
- Exactly three status marks — filled, outlined, muted — reused for doses, delivery,
  steps, radios and checkboxes.
- Fully round buttons and chips; 16px cards; 6px inputs. Rounder than a form, softer than
  a dashboard.
- Two warm shadows; borders and tone-steps do most of the depth work.
- Settling motion, 150ms for state and 340ms for entrances, never springy;
  `prefers-reduced-motion` collapses everything.
- Density that matches the moment: same screen, calm and loud renderings.

## Colors

A cool cornflower blue on a warm cream ground; every other hue is a status voice layered
onto a shaped, labelled mark.

### Primary
- **Cornflower** (`accent`): the one accent. Active tab bar item (icon, caption and the
  3px bar that hangs from the top edge), focus rings (2px outline, 2px offset), the lifted
  i-dot in the wordmark, the `rule` card's left mark, the accent button variant, the meter
  fill, inline "Open" links.
- **Cornflower Light** (`accent-2`): the accent button's hover state.
- **Cornflower Wash** (`accent-soft`): the fill behind an outlined accent Tag.

### Neutral
The warm cream ramp, dark to light — the project's own tonal scale:
- **Ink** (`ink`): body text, primary buttons, filled marks, the active sidebar row.
- **Soft Ink** (`ink-soft`): primary button hover.
- **Strong Mut** (`muted-strong`): secondary text, inactive nav, disabled input text — the
  darkest grey allowed for meaningful copy on cream (7.06:1 on `surface`).
- **Mut** (`muted`): timestamps, attributions and dashes. ⚠ AS-BUILT: currently also used
  for placeholders and some 13–15px body copy across ~50 sites, where it measures
  3.44–3.89:1 and fails WCAG AA. The intended boundary is below, under The Legible Grey Rule.
- **Empty Fill** (`fill-empty`): the muted/upcoming status mark, unknown meter cells.
- **Fill** (`fill`): skeleton bars, meter tracks, sidebar hover wash (`fill/60`), row
  hover wash (`fill/50`).
- **Strong Line** (`line-strong`): card borders, input borders, the ghost quotation glyph,
  neutral quote spines, the day thread's spine.
- **Line** (`line`): dividers, shell borders (sidebar, top bar, tab bar, sticky footers),
  link hover washes (`line/40`).
- **Canvas** (`canvas`): the app ground, set on `body`, and the `theme-color` meta.
- **Surface** (`surface`): default card background, sidebar, top bar, tab bar, sticky
  footers.
- **Paper** (`paper`): the brightest step — inputs, chips, the patient card, the
  `border`-emphasis card, hollow status marks.

### Status
- **Taken Blue** (`ok`): confirmed/taken/done. Deliberately the same hex as the accent —
  there is no green anywhere in this system.
- **Amber** (`warn`) on **Amber Wash** (`warn-soft`): watch severity, `no answer` doses,
  P2 badges. Dark enough to pass as text on cream (5.41:1 on `surface`).
- **Rose Red** (`danger`) on **Rose Wash** (`danger-soft`): red severity, missed doses, P1
  badges, the alert card's left mark and ground.
- **Marker Yellow** (`highlight`): the `::selection` background app-wide, and the
  transcript highlighter for a triggering line.

### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** One blue. Emphasis comes from weight, border thickness and
whitespace, not from new hues. If a surface feels flat, add space or a border — never a
colour.

**The Shape-First Status Rule.** Colour is layered onto a mark that already carries shape
*and* a text label. Every screen must survive DevTools → Rendering → Emulate vision
deficiency → Achromatopsia, and a greyscale screen recording.

**The No-Green Rule.** "Taken" is the accent blue (`ok` ≡ `accent`), never green. Good
news in this system is calm, not celebratory.

**The Traceable Severity Rule.** Severity is `red` / `watch` / `none`, each traceable to a
sentence the parent said. No score, gauge, index, ring or sentiment bar exists or may be
added.

**The Legible Grey Rule.** `muted-strong` is the floor for anything a user has to read —
body copy, secondary lines, placeholders, disabled labels. `muted` is for decoration and
non-essential metadata only. On this cream ramp `muted` never clears 3.9:1; the caregiver
reading it is often presbyopic and always in a hurry.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Newsreader (with Georgia, 'Times New Roman', serif)
**Body Font:** IBM Plex Sans (with system-ui, -apple-system, 'Segoe UI', Roboto)
**Devanagari Font:** IBM Plex Sans Devanagari (bound via `:lang(hi)`, `:lang(mr)`,
`:lang(pa)` — with IBM Plex Sans fallback)

**Character:** A plain-spoken sans for the record-keeping, and a literary serif that
appears only when the content is the care itself. The serif reads as "her voice and her
medicines"; the sans reads as "the system's bookkeeping."

### Hierarchy
The scale is tokenized in `@theme`, each step carrying its own line-height:
2xs 11/1.35 · xs 13/1.45 · sm 14/1.5 · base 15/1.55 · md 17/1.45 · lg 20/1.3 · xl 24/1.25 ·
2xl 30/1.15 · 3xl 40/1.08 · 4xl 56/1.03 (px).

- **Display** (600, 40px `text-3xl`, Newsreader): the single hero line per screen — the
  next medicine's name on Home. The largest thing on the screen it appears on.
- **Headline** (600, 30px `text-2xl`, Newsreader): the reassurance line when every dose is
  accounted for; hero quotes where `QuoteBlock` is used.
- **Title** (700, 17px `text-md`, IBM Plex Sans): the lead line of a card — the alert
  reason, the empty-state title, an open alert's headline.
- **Body** (400, 15px `text-base`, IBM Plex Sans): default, set on `body`. Secondary body
  drops to 14px `text-sm` in `muted-strong`; metadata to 13px `text-xs`.
- **Label** (700, 11px `text-2xs`, 0.08em tracking, UPPERCASE, `muted-strong`): section
  labels, timestamps in card headers, tab bar captions. 11px exists *only* for these —
  never body copy.

### Named Rules
**The Earned Serif Rule.** Newsreader appears only on care content: verbatim quotes,
medicine names, the one hero line per screen, and the wordmark. It is never decoration,
never a routine screen title, never sprinkled.

**The Verbatim Rule.** What the parent said renders exactly, wrapped in real quotation
marks (`“ ”`), never summarised, paraphrased, or restyled into a log line. Hindi/Marathi
quotes carry a real `lang` attribute so they set in Plex Devanagari — and only when the
text actually is in that language, since `lang` also changes the screen reader's voice.

**The Tabular Time Rule.** Times, durations and counts take `.tnum`
(`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) so they never jitter as they tick.

## Layout

Mobile-first at 390pt; desktop begins at 1024px (`DESKTOP_MIN_PX` in `app/src/config.ts`,
deliberately equal to Tailwind's `lg:` so the shell and the screens switch on the same
pixel). One shell, two layouts, identical screens in both:

- **Phone:** scrolling `<main>` with 16px padding (`p-4`), and a bottom tab bar
  (`surface/95`, `line` top border, `backdrop-blur`, `env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`
  padding) with exactly four tabs — Home · Calendar · Alerts · Calls. Never a fifth. Each
  tab is at least 56px tall.
- **Desktop:** a 208px left sidebar (`surface`, `line` right border, 16px padding) and a
  56px top bar (`h-14`, `surface/80`, `backdrop-blur`, `line` bottom border, 24px
  padding). `<main>` gets 24px padding (`p-6`).
- **Sidebar order:** Wordmark (17px) → the live patient card → the seven nav rows (four
  tabs plus Care record, What she said, Settings) → `mt-auto` → Log out, pinned to the
  bottom.

Content columns cap at `max-w-5xl`. Home uses a `1.4fr / 1fr` two-column grid at `lg:`;
the open alert card, when present, renders above the grid and spans the full width.

Vertical rhythm: 16px (`gap-4`) between Home's top-level cards, 12px (`gap-3`) between
cards in the setup flow, 8–10px inside a card. Related rows share one surface; unrelated
content is separated by space, not by another border. Fewer, larger surfaces beat many
small boxes.

**Surfaces outside the shell.** Login, the four setup steps and the handoff render with no
sidebar and no tab bar, each with its own measure: Login `max-w-md` at 20px padding,
Parent `max-w-2xl`, Consent `max-w-3xl`, Handoff `max-w-[430px]` on a `paper` ground.

**Sticky CTA bar.** Multi-step forms end in a `sticky bottom-0` footer on `surface` with a
`line` top border, bleeding through `<main>`'s padding with negative margins so it sits
flush on the scrollport edge, and `padding-bottom: max(0.75rem, env(safe-area-inset-bottom))`
so the CTA clears the home bar. Keep the bleed in step with `<main>`'s padding (16px phone,
24px desktop) or the footer will inset or overflow.

**The One Thing Leads Rule.** Each screen has exactly one element that leads — the next
dose on Home, the P1 alert when one is open (it outranks the dose), the quote on an
observation. A stranger should know where to look in under a second.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is mostly *material*: stepped cream tones (canvas → surface → paper) and hairline
borders. Shadows are a whisper on top, warm-tinted with a brown-ink base
(`rgba(58, 44, 20, …)`) so they never go blue-grey on cream. Two tokens only, and both are
in use:

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`--shadow-card`: `0 1px 2px rgba(58, 44, 20, 0.05), 0 2px 10px rgba(58, 44, 20, 0.05)`):
  every Card at rest, plus the sidebar's patient card. The default and near-universal
  shadow.
- **Lift** (`--shadow-lift`: `0 6px 16px rgba(58, 44, 20, 0.1), 0 14px 40px rgba(58, 44, 20, 0.1)`):
  reserved for the single card that leads a screen — carried by `Card emphasis="border"`,
  which is how the next-dose card lifts off the grid.
- Cards transition their shadow over 200ms, so a card can lift on state change without
  moving.

The two chrome bars (top bar, tab bar) use a translucent `surface` plus `backdrop-blur`
rather than a shadow, so content dissolves under them instead of casting onto them.

### Named Rules
**The Two Warm Shadows Rule.** No new shadow values. If a surface needs more presence,
step its tone (surface → paper), thicken its border, or give it a left mark — do not invent
a third drop shadow.

**The Lift Is Singular Rule.** `--shadow-lift` marks the one card that leads. Two lifted
cards on one screen means neither leads.

## Shapes

Radius carries hierarchy, and it is softer than a record-keeping product usually goes:

| Role | Radius | Class |
|---|---|---|
| Buttons, chips, status dots, meter bar, the tab bar's active mark | full | `rounded-full` |
| Cards | 16px | `rounded-2xl` |
| Sidebar nav rows, the patient card | 12px | `rounded-xl` |
| `Field`, `Placeholder`, inline row hovers | 8px | `rounded-lg` |
| Inputs, selects, OTP boxes, Tag badges, small icon buttons | 6px | `rounded-md` |
| Intake meter cells | 3px | `rounded-[3px]` |

Borders are 1px `line-strong` by default; emphasis raises them to 1.5px `ink`, never a new
colour. Dividers are 1px `line`.

The signature silhouette is the **left mark**: a 4px left border carrying state — accent
on a `rule` card, danger on a `danger` card — set against a 1px border on the other three
sides. `QuoteBlock` carries the same idea as a 3px rounded severity spine.

**The One Left Mark Rule.** A card carries at most one left mark. Nested content that has
its own spine (a `QuoteBlock`) sits in a card without one.

The hairline is the brand's geometry: the wordmark's underline, the day thread's 1px spine
(dots punch through it with a 3px `surface` outline), and `Divider`.

## Components

All primitives live in `app/src/ui/index.tsx`; signature components in
`app/src/ui/signature.tsx` (re-exported, so screens keep one import). Icons are Lucide
strokes, 16–22px, `strokeWidth` 2 rising to 2.4–2.5 when active, always beside a text
label.

### Buttons
Confident, quiet capsules — ink first, blue only when the accent earns it.
- **Shape:** fully round (`rounded-full`), 20px × 10px padding, 14px semibold, `min-height: 44px`.
- **Primary:** `ink` fill, `paper` text; hover `ink-soft`.
- **Accent:** `accent` fill, white text; hover `accent-2`.
- **Outline:** transparent with a 1.5px `ink` border; hover washes `ink/5`.
- **Motion:** `active:scale-[0.98]`, 150ms on `--ease-out`, colours and transform only.
- **Call buttons — the Handoff Rule:** the app never dials. A call action is a real
  `<a href="tel:…">` styled as a Button, naming who it will dial ("Call Amma"). It is a
  handoff to the OS dialler, not an in-app call.
- **Disabled:** ⚠ AS-BUILT `opacity-40` + `cursor-not-allowed`. The intended treatment is
  **Warm Inert** — a `fill` ground with readable `muted-strong` text and the reason in
  plain text beside it ("2 left", "needs the Care API") — because a gated button must stay
  legible, and PRODUCT.md requires the reason to be adjacent. Prefer Warm Inert in new work.
- **Internal links:** ⚠ AS-BUILT `Button` accepts only `href`, so an internal route
  rendered through it is a full page load. Use react-router `Link` for in-app navigation;
  keep `href` for `tel:`.

### Chips
- **Style:** fully round, `paper` ground, 1px `line-strong` border, 13px medium, 34px tall,
  14px horizontal padding.
- **State:** selected (`on`) inverts to `ink` fill / `paper` text; interactive chips get
  `aria-pressed`, hover raises the border to `ink`, and `active:scale-[0.97]`.
- Chips are also used non-interactively as read-only pills ("Transcript",
  "Metformin · confirmed").

### Tags (badges)
- **Style:** 6px radius, 11px extrabold with wide tracking, 6px × 2px padding. Filled
  (tone fill, white/paper text) or outlined (1px tone border on the tone's soft wash).
- **Roles:** P1 = filled `danger`, P2 = filled `warn`, `watch` = outlined `warn`, `red` =
  filled `danger`, `priority` = filled `accent`, slot names = outlined `accent`.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px; internal padding 16px, internal gap 10px.
- **Background:** `surface` with a 1px `line-strong` border and the card shadow.
- **Emphases (exactly four, and the names are exact):**
  - `none` (or omitted) — the default surface card.
  - `border` — 1.5px `ink` border on `paper` with `--shadow-lift`: the "this leads" card.
  - `rule` — 1px `line-strong` border with a 4px `accent` left mark on `surface`.
  - `danger` — `danger/35` border with a 4px `danger` left mark on `danger-soft`: the loud
    bad-day card.
  - ⚠ AS-BUILT: `Alerts.tsx` passes `emphasis="alert"`, which is not one of these. No
    branch matches it and the card renders with no ground, no border and no shadow. Use
    `danger`.
- **Empty states:** the same card with a dashed border, centred — a 20px Newsreader title,
  capped 13px body in `muted-strong`, and an optional action.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** `paper` ground, 1px `line-strong` border, 6px radius, 15–17px `ink` text,
  10px × 8px padding, full width.
- **Focus:** the app-wide ring — `*:focus-visible { outline: 2px solid accent; outline-offset: 2px }`
  declared unlayered in `index.css`, so it applies over any utility. Text inputs add
  `focus:border-ink` as a second cue.
- **Placeholders:** `muted-strong`. ⚠ AS-BUILT three input groups still use `muted`;
  see The Legible Grey Rule.
- **Read-only twin:** `Field` renders a stored value in the same frame at 8px radius,
  falling back to a placeholder when empty.
- **OTP boxes:** six equal `rounded-md` `paper` boxes, centred 20px semibold, sharing one
  `role="group"`; each carries `aria-label="<label>, digit N"`, and `disabled` shifts the
  ground to `surface`.
- **Checkboxes:** native `<input type="checkbox">` with `accent-ink`, paired with a real
  `<label htmlFor>` — the consent gate is a real disabled control, never a warning.

### Status Marks
Exactly three marks, always beside a text label, reused for doses, delivery, steps, radios
and checkboxes. All are 10px round dots:
- **Filled** (solid `ink` / `accent` / `danger` / `warn`) = taken / done / confirmed /
  consent given.
- **Outlined** (`paper` ground, 2px border in the tone) = missed / negative / needs action.
- **Muted** (`fill-empty`) = upcoming / pending / locked / deferred.

`DoseStatusChip` maps the four dose states: confirmed → filled `accent` + "taken"; missed →
outlined `danger` + semibold "missed"; no_answer → outlined `warn` + semibold "no answer"
(unreachable is unknown, never missed); deferred → muted + "deferred". `SeverityChip`: red →
filled `danger` Tag; watch → outlined `warn` Tag; none → the word "noted" as a Label.

### Navigation
- **Tab bar (mobile):** four items on `surface/95` with `backdrop-blur`, each ≥56px tall —
  a 22px icon above an 11px caption. Active = `accent` + bold caption + `strokeWidth` 2.5 +
  a 3px `accent` bar hanging from the top edge of the item; inactive = `muted-strong` +
  `strokeWidth` 2.
- **Sidebar (desktop):** 12px-radius rows, 14px text, 18px icons. Active = `ink` fill with
  `paper` text and `strokeWidth` 2.4; hover = `fill/60` wash and `ink` text. `NavLink`
  supplies `aria-current="page"` on the active row.
- **Log out:** pinned to the sidebar's bottom with `mt-auto`, styled as an inactive nav row
  with a `LogOut` glyph. It ends the server session, then drops every cached query except
  the session itself — the care record belongs to the caregiver who just left and must not
  flash for the next one.
- **Top bar (desktop):** the wordmark set in Newsreader at 20px beside a 13px
  `muted-strong` line, on `surface/80` with `backdrop-blur`.

### Page States
- **Loading — the Skeleton Rule:** `LoadingBlock` renders `Bar` skeletons (10px tall,
  shimmering `fill`, stepping widths) in the final layout's shape, with `aria-busy`,
  `aria-live="polite"` and an sr-only "Loading". Never a spinner; nothing shifts when data
  lands.
- **Error:** `ErrorBlock` is a `danger`-emphasis card — a "Not loaded" Label, the message in
  semibold, the reassurance that the record itself is safe, and an outline "Try again".
- **Empty:** `EmptyBlock` — dashed card, Newsreader title, one capped line of body, optional
  action.
- **Meter:** the same `Bar` takes a `fill` fraction (`accent` on `fill`, animating its width
  over 500ms) — the only gauge allowed, and it measures completeness, never wellbeing.

### Motion
- **State:** 150ms on `--ease-out` (`cubic-bezier(0.22, 0.61, 0.36, 1)`), colours and
  transform only. Cards cross-fade their shadow over 200ms.
- **Entrance:** `.kv-rise` — 340ms, 10px up with a fade, on `--ease-out`. Used on Home's
  cards so the screen settles rather than snaps.
- **Skeletons:** `.kv-shimmer` — a 1.4s `--ease-soft` sweep across a `fill`-to-`surface`
  gradient.
- **Reduced motion:** `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` sets both animations to
  `none` and collapses transitions. Never springy, never above 340ms.

### Wordmark (signature)
"Kinvox" in Newsreader with a 1px hairline running under the full word and a 3px `accent`
dot sitting on the line where the i's dot belongs — the call, drawn. 17px in the sidebar,
26px on the login screen; scalable via a `size` prop, with the dot placed at 38% of the
size.

### QuoteBlock (signature)
Her words as the largest thing on the screen: a 3px rounded severity spine (danger / warn /
`line-strong`), an oversized ghost `“` (76px lg / 54px sm, Newsreader, `line-strong`)
behind the text, the quote itself in Newsreader light (30px lg / 20px sm), and a 13px

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give every status a shape and a text label first, then layer colour on top — and
  verify under achromatopsia emulation and in greyscale.
- **Do** render parent quotes verbatim, in real quotation marks, sized as the most
  important text on the screen, with `lang` set only when the text really is Hindi or
  Marathi.
- **Do** use `muted-strong` for anything the user must read; reserve `muted` for
  decoration and non-essential metadata.
- **Do** show the literal rule string on alerts (`rule: chest complaint with age over 40`)
  under a P1/P2 badge, and keep the sentence "No interpretation, no diagnosis" on screen.
- **Do** keep gated buttons visible with the reason adjacent ("2 left", "All three are
  mandatory"), and prefer the Warm Inert treatment over an opacity ghost.
- **Do** style call actions as OS handoffs: real `tel:` links naming who they dial. Route
  in-app navigation through react-router `Link`, never a bare `href`.
- **Do** load with skeletons in the final layout's shape; nothing may shift when data lands.
- **Do** keep entrances at 340ms and state changes at 150ms on `--ease-out` /
  `--ease-soft`, and let `prefers-reduced-motion` collapse them.
- **Do** keep touch targets ≥44px and times/counts in `.tnum`.
- **Do** keep the sticky-footer bleed in step with `<main>`'s padding — 16px on a phone,
  24px at `lg:`.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a hue. One accent; emphasis is weight, border and whitespace. No green —
  "taken" is blue.
- **Don't** invent a score, gauge, mood percentage, sentiment bar, wellbeing ring or index.
  Severity is red/watch/none, each traceable to a sentence. Feel the urge to add a gauge →
  add whitespace.
- **Don't** add a fourth status mark, a fifth mobile tab, a third shadow, or a second left
  mark on one card.
- **Don't** pass a `Card` emphasis outside `none` / `border` / `rule` / `danger` — an
  unrecognised value silently strips the card of all styling.
- **Don't** use Newsreader outside care content (quotes, medicine names, the one hero line,
  the wordmark), or 11px for anything but labels and tab captions.
- **Don't** use spinners, in-app dialling affordances, springy easings, or placeholder text
  of any kind (no lorem ipsum, no TODO).
- **Don't** hardcode a hex. Every colour goes through a `@theme` token class.
- **Don't** rename or delete `@theme` tokens; extend, don't replace.
